Support

Milk Voice WF Tools · Help, bug reports and common questions

Get help

Email patrykmalecki2k@gmail.com with your question, a bug, or what you think of the app. There is no account or ticket system: it goes straight to me, Milk Voice, the person who makes the app.

Report a bug

  1. Open the app and go to Support in the sidebar.
  2. Under "Report a bug", describe what you did and what you expected to happen.
  3. Press Copy report, then paste it into an email to the address above.

The report holds the app and system versions, how long the app has been running, and the latest warnings and errors it logged. Your Windows user name and folders are removed from it. The app does not send the report anywhere: you decide where to paste it.

Common questions

How do I show or hide the app while I play?

Press Ctrl + Alt + F9 from anywhere. It brings the app forward, and pressing it again minimizes it. You can change the keys in the app under Settings, Hotkey.

Does the app read Warframe or change the game?

It never changes the game, and it never touches the game's files or memory itself. While Warframe is running, Overwolf gives the app your account name, your inventory, the item you are looking at in the game, and chat messages. They stay on your PC: the app does not send them anywhere, chat is kept in memory only (not saved), and chat is not shown anywhere yet. The last inventory is kept on your PC so you can still look at it after Warframe closes; you can clear it in Settings, under "Your saved data". The rest of the app uses public web data: Warframe's world state feed, Warframe Market (prices and mod pictures), and item pictures from GitHub.

My inventory is empty.

Inventory comes from the game through Overwolf, so Warframe has to be running, and Overwolf sends it once you load into a mission (any mission, even a short one). Overwolf works best when this app is already open before you start the game: start the app first, then Warframe. If Warframe runs as administrator, Overwolf cannot read it; start both normally.

Why does the app show ads and ask about privacy?

The app runs on Overwolf, which pays for free apps with ads. Where the law requires it, you choose what Overwolf and its partners may use. In the app, open Settings, Privacy, and press Manage to see or change your choices. The Privacy Policy explains the details.

How are the Trends worked out?

Each item’s price over the last 7 (or 30) days is compared with the 7 (or 30) days before, using the daily prices and trade counts from Warframe Market. Only the most traded version of an item is used (for example one mod rank), a move of less than 2% counts as steady, and an item needs enough trades and a price of at least 10p to be ranked. Trends cover a list of busy items plus the items on your watchlist, not the whole market. The prices are saved on your PC and refreshed after 12 hours.

How is a relic's worth worked out?

Open Inventory, then Relics. The worth of a relic is the platinum you can expect from opening one of it at its refinement: each reward's chance (from the community drop data) times its recent price on Warframe Market, added up. Rewards that cannot be traded count as nothing. Prices are fetched in the background, a few a second, and kept on your PC for 12 hours. While some are still missing the value is shown as a minimum. It is an estimate for your platform, not a promise: real sale prices vary.

Where do the item pictures come from?

From the community item database WFCD/warframe-items on GitHub. The app downloads the picture of an item the first time it is shown in your Inventory and then keeps it on your PC. If a picture cannot be downloaded (you are offline, or it does not exist), the tile shows a plain icon for its category instead. The artwork belongs to Digital Extremes.

Where is my data kept?

On your PC. Your checklist, watchlist and settings are saved by the app and are never sent anywhere. In the app, use Settings, Your saved data, to clear them.

A timer or the world state looks wrong.

The world state comes from Warframe's feed and can lag for a minute or two after a reset. Press refresh in the World panel. If it stays wrong, send a bug report as described above.
