Privacy Policy

Milk Voice WF Tools · Last updated 30 October 2026

1. Who this is about

Milk Voice WF Tools ("the app") is made by Milk Voice ("I", "me"). You can reach me at patrykmalecki2k@gmail.com. The app is unofficial and is not connected to Digital Extremes.

2. The short version

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. I do not run a server that receives your personal information, and the app does not send me your data. What the app keeps stays on your own PC. Three outside parties are involved: Overwolf (the platform the app runs on, which shows ads and counts anonymous usage), Warframe Market (prices) and Warframe's public world state feed (timers and events).

3. What stays on your PC

The app saves your own choices in its data folder: settings, your watchlist and price alerts, checklist ticks and your own checklist items, recent searches, your hotkey and window preferences, and the last Warframe inventory the app received (see section 4). This is stored only on your PC. You can clear most of it in Settings, under "Your saved data". It stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app leaves the data folder in place, so you can delete that folder yourself to remove everything.

4. Warframe game data (through Overwolf)

While Warframe is running, and in versions of the app where Overwolf game data is switched on, Overwolf provides the app with:

  • your Warframe account name,
  • your inventory (the items and amounts you own),
  • the item you are currently looking at in the game (including Riven details, if any), and
  • chat messages that appear in the game's chat.

The app uses this only on your PC, to show your name, the Inventory page and the "Viewing in game" card. It does not send this information to me or to any server. So that you can still look at your Inventory after Warframe closes, the app keeps the last inventory it received (your items and amounts, what is in your Foundry, and your credits, platinum and endo) in its data folder on your PC, until a newer one arrives or you clear it in Settings, under "Your saved data". Your name, the item you were viewing and chat are not kept. Chat messages are held only in the app's memory, are never saved to disk and are lost when you close the app or the game. Overwolf collects and provides this data under its own terms.

5. Network requests the app makes

To work, the app contacts these services. They can see your IP address and the request itself, as with any website you visit:

  • Warframe Market (api.warframe.market, and item pictures from warframe.market): item lists, prices and price history for the items you look at, for a list of busy items shown on the Trends page (about 120 items, plus the items on your watchlist), riven listings for the weapon you search on the Rivens page, and, when you open Relics in your Inventory, recent prices for the items in your relics. Mod card pictures shown in the Inventory are loaded from warframe.market. The app saves the price history it received on your PC and refreshes it after 12 hours. No account details are ever sent. The app identifies itself as "MilkVoiceWFTools".
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, the WFCD/warframe-items project): the Inventory's item pictures. The first time a picture of an item you own is shown, the app downloads it from GitHub and then keeps it on your PC, so each picture is downloaded once. GitHub can see your IP address and which picture files are requested, which tells it roughly which kinds of items you own; no account details are sent.
  • Warframe's public world state feed (api.warframe.com): the timers and events shown in the World panel and used by the checklist.
  • Overwolf: downloads of its game-data packages, ads and usage statistics, described next.

6. Overwolf, ads and consent

The app runs on the Overwolf platform. Overwolf shows advertising in the app and collects anonymous usage statistics, such as when the app starts and when its windows are shown. Overwolf and its advertising partners may store or access information on your device and process data such as your IP address and cookies to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), the app asks for your choice first, and you can review or change it at any time under Settings, Privacy, Manage.

Overwolf gives me summary statistics about the app (for example how many people use it and how ads perform). I do not receive information that identifies you. Overwolf's own privacy policy explains what it collects and why: legal.overwolf.com/docs/overwolf/platform/platform-privacy-policy.

7. Other things the app does on your PC

  • Music widget: the app reads what your media player (for example Spotify) is playing from Windows, and can send play, pause, skip and volume commands. This happens on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Clipboard: the app copies text (such as a trade whisper or a support report) only when you press a copy button.
  • Picture cache: the item pictures downloaded for your Inventory are kept in a folder called item-icons inside the app's data folder, so each is downloaded once. You can delete that folder at any time; the pictures are downloaded again when needed.
  • Notifications: price alerts and world alerts can show Windows notifications.
  • Support report: the Support page can copy a short report (app and system versions, recent errors). Your Windows user name and folders are removed from it. The app does not send it anywhere: you choose whether to send it to me.

8. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad and privacy choices any time under Settings, Privacy, Manage.
  • Clear your saved data in Settings, or delete the app's data folder.
  • Because I hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for me to give you or delete. If you are in the UK or EU you have rights under data protection law (such as access and deletion) over the data other parties hold. For data held by Overwolf, contact Overwolf. If you have any question, write to me.
